USS Colorado named first boat of Submarine Rotational Force-West

The Virginia-class submarine USS Colorado was shown to media at Garden Island on Wednesday as the first submarine assigned to Submarine Rotational Force-West. It will be based at HMAS Stirling as part of an upskilling programme for the local workforce ahead of Australia's own acquisition of nuclear-powered submarines. Jennifer Parker, adjunct professor at UWA's Defence and Security Institute, said Stirling "would be some kind of target" in a major Indo-Pacific conflict while calling that scenario unlikely, and pointed to the economic lift for Western Australia.

Inflation accelerates to 4.3 percent, hardening expectations of a November rate rise

The consumer price index rose 0.8 percent in the month of September, from 0.4 percent in August, taking annual inflation to 4.3 percent from 4 percent, the Australian Bureau of Statistics said. Housing was the largest contributor at 5.2 percent and fuel prices rose 9.4 percent; the trimmed mean lifted to 3.6 percent against an August reading revised down to 3.5 percent. Market pricing for a 0.25 percent increase next Tuesday moved to about 85 percent on Bloomberg data, and Jim Chalmers called for "perspective", pointing to external factors and lowering expectations of further fuel discounts.
