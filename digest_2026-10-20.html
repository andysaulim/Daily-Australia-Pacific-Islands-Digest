Pacific-Diplomacy

ADF opens recruitment to PNG university students under Pukpuk Treaty

The Australian Defence Force will open recruitment to Papua New Guinean university students from next month under a $65 million trial, announced at a ministerial meeting in Port Moresby. The pilot aims to recruit 100 young Papua New Guineans over two years, with the first cohort expected to be sworn in by late 2027. Recruits will enlist on temporary visas and must eventually obtain Australian citizenship to continue serving. The Pukpuk Treaty, in force since July, lets each country recruit the other's citizens and obliges both to defend each other if attacked.

per ABC News (Marian Faa): "Australian military opens its ranks to PNG university students" · also The Australian
NZ-Defense

New Zealand signs $1.6 billion deal for five Seahawk helicopters

New Zealand signed a NZ$1.6 billion agreement with the US government for five Seahawk maritime helicopters, anti-submarine torpedoes, and air-to-surface missiles, one of the biggest single purchases so far under the Defence Capability Plan. Washington approved the sale in June under its Foreign Military Sale system. The Lockheed Martin subsidiary-built aircraft arrive at Whenuapai air base from 2030 and will cost an additional $110 million a year to operate, with further funding needed to upgrade the base. The NZDF cited Australia's operation of Seahawks as a factor.
