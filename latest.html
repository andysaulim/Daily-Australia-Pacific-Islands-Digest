Australia gifts Bluebottle drone vessels to Fiji as Pacific drug seizures surge

Australia is donating an unspecified number of Bluebottle uncrewed surface vessels to Fiji under the $600 million Pacific-led, Australian-supported Waqa Moana maritime security initiative, with the first expected next year. The Australian Federal Police say 17 tonnes of illicit drugs, mostly cocaine, have been seized in the Pacific since January, against about 4.6 tonnes for all of 2025. Fiji's Defence Minister Pio Tikoduadua said the country's waters are too large to watch with its two Australian-donated Guardian-class patrol boats, RFNS Savenaca and RFNS Timo. Vessel numbers are being kept vague deliberately.

Transparency win reopens fight over Australia's 2004 Timor-Leste bugging operation

Former senator Rex Patrick has won a ruling advancing his campaign to obtain documents on the 2004 mission in which Australian spies allegedly bugged Timor-Leste during oil and gas negotiations. The operation became a public scandal years later when a former intelligence officer and a former attorney-general attempted to mount a case in The Hague. Patrick describes it as the most open secret in the history of Australian spying.

Fiji's NFP directs MPs to vote against Constitution Bill at third reading
