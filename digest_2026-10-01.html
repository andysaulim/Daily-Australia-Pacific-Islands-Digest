Pacific-Diplomacy

American Samoa group sues Washington over seabed mining lease sale

Fa'asao Amerika Samoa and the Conservation Council for Hawaii filed suit in August challenging a US plan to lease more than 31 million acres of seabed around American Samoa, with the sale scheduled for November 19 and initial leases running 20 years. The complaint says the United States has never before opened its waters to deep-sea mining. Spokesperson Seumalu Elora Raymond said regulators had not adequately assessed risks to whales, turtles and sharks.

per RNZ Pacific: "American Samoa environmental group sues US govt over deep sea mining plans"

Fiji locks in strict campaign rules ahead of 2027 poll deadline

Fiji's Electoral Commission has adopted the Campaign Limitation Rules 2026, barring the use of taxpayer-funded and state resources for campaigning and restricting where campaign materials may be displayed, effective October 20. Eleven parties, the most since Fiji's 2014 return to democratic rule, are confirmed to contest the election, which must be held by February 6, 2027. More than 723,000 voters were registered as of June 30.
