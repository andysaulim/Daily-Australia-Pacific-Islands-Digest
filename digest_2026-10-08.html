US sanctions Fiji businessman for 'significant corruption' tied to China

The US State Department designated Zhao Fugang, director of Fiji's Overseas Chinese Service Center and owner of Suva's Yue Lai Hotel, for "significant corruption" on behalf of China-based actors. The designation bars Zhao and his immediate family from entering the United States. His lawyers told RNZ Pacific the allegations are "unsubstantiated without any supporting evidence." A 2024 investigation by the Organised Crime and Corruption Reporting Project linked him to a syndicate accused of drug smuggling, money laundering, and human trafficking.

US-Australia

Missed UPS email let China seize F-35 parts shipped from Australia

A shipment of parts from the F-35 stealth fighter, sent from Australia, was seized by China after a UPS worker missed an email, the Australian Financial Review reported. The consignment had been routed toward San Francisco via South Korea and Taiwan. The incident puts a commercial logistics handover at the centre of a question about how allied defence components move between AUKUS and Indo-Pacific partners.

Trade-Economy

Ground broken on $1.8 billion rare earths mine backed by Washington and Canberra

Arafura Rare Earths broke ground at its Nolans project 135 kilometres north of Alice Springs, a year after the US and Australian governments signed a critical minerals deal. The mine and processing plant are promised within three years, with a projected life of nearly 40 years. NT Chief Minister Lia Finocchiaro said the project mattered "not just for the Territory and Australia, but for our global allies and partners." Gina Rinehart is among the backers.

Taylor and Abbott tie migration crackdown to Bondi attack

Opposition Leader Angus Taylor and former prime minister Tony Abbott linked the Coalition's migration clampdown to the Bondi massacre.
