RBA lifts cash rate to 4.6 percent and warns of more to come

The Reserve Bank raised its benchmark rate to 4.6 percent, the fourth increase this year and the highest level in 15 years. Governor Michele Bullock said the decision was not taken lightly and flagged further rises even if they tip Australia into recession, pointing to domestic pressures alongside the energy supply shock. The AFR reports Australia now carries the second-highest cash rate among advanced economies and one of the highest inflation rates.
