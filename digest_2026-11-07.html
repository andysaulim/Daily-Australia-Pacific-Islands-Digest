AU-Defense

Defence to sell sixty-seven sites, stripping Tasmania of live-fire ranges

The Department of Defence will sell sixty-seven sites nationwide to raise about $1.8 billion, among them Tasmania's two live fire ranges, the 5,500-hectare Stony Head Training Area near Launceston and the Buckland Military Training Area. Peter Williams, a veteran and secretary of the Launceston RSL, said Stony Head is one of the best amphibious training beaches in Australia. The sales will leave the state without a live fire training area.
