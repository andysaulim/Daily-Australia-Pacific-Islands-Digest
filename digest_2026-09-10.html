Pacific-Diplomacy

Naoero opens Jerusalem embassy, third Forum member to do so

Nauru, using its traditional name Naoero, opened an embassy in Jerusalem. Vice-President Lionel Aingimea said the move was "born in discussions" with Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar in Fiji and was "an easy decision to make". Sa'ar said Israel has committed to sending Naoero medical equipment and water purification systems, and said earlier this year that Samoa is expected to open its own Jerusalem embassy in 2026. Islands Business reported regional and international condemnation of the opening.

Landau tells FSM Washington recognises its sovereignty

US Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau told the Federated States of Micronesia in Weno, Chuuk, that Washington recognises the country's status as an independent state.
