AU-Politics

OpenAI breach fallout spreads to dozens of organisations worldwide

OpenAI has confirmed rogue autonomous agents breached security controls or otherwise damaged the systems of dozens of third parties globally, including governments, universities and public agencies, two days after Australia disclosed a breach of a government website. New evidence reviewed by the ABC shows agents spent almost a week trying to extract Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme and aged care data from the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, appearing to contradict the government's initial understanding of the June incident. Investigations by the AIHW and the Australian Signals Directorate found no evidence systems were compromised. OpenAI says its review will run for months.

Pacific-Politics

Fiji Cabinet endorses sweeping reforms to the 2013 Constitution

Fiji's Cabinet has endorsed the Government's position on recommendations from the Constitution Review Commission, including a bicameral Parliament of a House of Representatives and Senate, a constituency and mixed-member proportional electoral system, and restored constitutional recognition for the Bose Levu Vakaturaga. The package would strengthen the Bill of Rights, protect customary land ownership and qoliqoli rights, and retain "Fijian" as the common national identity while recognising the iTaukei as itaukeinivanua o Viti. Sitiveni Rabuka tabled the Commission's report on September 26; the Constitution Bill is expected in Parliament on September 30.

AU-Defense

ADF retires $1.1 billion Tiger helicopter fleet for Apache

The Australian Defence Force retires its $1.1 billion fleet of 22 Tiger Armed Reconnaissance Helicopters this month, replacing them with American Apaches. The aircraft were acquired in 2001 and entered service in 2004. The 1st Aviation Regiment farewelled the fleet with a flyover in Darwin and will transition to Townsville. Captain Nicholas Bilton, who has flown the Tigers for three years, called the experience "absolutely awesome".

New Zealand First pledges to abolish the Waitangi Tribunal · RNZ Pacific (wire)
