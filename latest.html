AUKUS

Marles rejects US budget office warning on AUKUS submarine deal

Richard Marles rejected a US Congressional Budget Office report warning that the sale of Virginia-class submarines to Australia could diminish the US Navy's attack submarine force through to 2060. "They are wrong," the deputy prime minister said in televised remarks, adding that AUKUS "is very much happening" and that the American production rate "is absolutely increasing". Canberra has committed $368 billion over three decades to the programme, Reuters reported.

AU-Foreign-Policy

Wong to declare AI a top national security challenge

Penny Wong will tell parliament on Tuesday that artificial intelligence is a "major foreign policy and national security challenge", warning that the technology hands foreign actors new tools to disrupt democracies. She will cite an Iranian operation using fake journalist personas to sway opinion on the US-Iran conflict, and Russian covert use of AI-generated deepfakes in US elections. The speech comes amid concern that AI data centres built in Australia could be vulnerable to foreign actors.

per ABC News: "Foreign minister to elevate AI as national security challenge"
Pacific-Diplomacy

Pacific Resilience Facility passes $200 million toward COP31 goal

The Pacific Resilience Facility launched its international capitalization campaign at COP31 in Antalya, crossing $200 million of a $500 million target, said Surangel Whipps Jr., Palau's president and chair of the Pacific Islands Forum. Of that, $100 million is Pacific money. Andrew Forrest's Minderoo Foundation pledged $5 million. "We should not have to pay for our own survival, but we act without hesitation," Whipps said, calling for the remaining $300 million before the next COP.
