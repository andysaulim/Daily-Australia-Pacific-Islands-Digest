Trade-Economy

Trump to decide tariffs on Australian lamb after US trade panel deadlocks

A tie vote at the US International Trade Commission leaves President Donald Trump to decide whether to impose tariffs on Australian lamb, after commissioners split on whether imports have harmed American producers. More than 100,000 tonnes of Australian lamb, worth over $1 billion, went to the United States last year. A public remedies hearing is set for December 1, with a report due to Trump by January 11. Georgetown University's Jennifer Hillman, a former USITC commissioner, said a tie lets the president pick the winning vote.

Australia and Philippines to sign defence pact amid South China Sea tensions

Australia and the Philippines are set to sign a defence cooperation agreement on Sunday as Anthony Albanese meets senior Philippine officials at the ASEAN summit in Manila. The pact is expected to expand reciprocal military access and information sharing and to bolster the Philippine Navy and coastguard. It advances the informal four-nation grouping nicknamed "the Squad," comprising Australia, the United States, the Philippines and Japan, whose defence officials met in Cebu last month and this week announced a Combined Coordination Centre.
