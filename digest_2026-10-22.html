Vanuatu minister says no decision made to cut Chinese police presence

Vanuatu's Internal Affairs Minister Andrew Napuat said his country has made no decision to reduce China's police presence, days after a joint Vanuatu-Australia statement described Beijing's police assistance program as "time-limited and expected to reduce over time." The statement, cleared after a high-level meeting on implementing the Nakamal Agreement, called Australia's own policing arrangements "legally binding" with "no expiry date." A federal government source said Napuat's message was "very different" from one conveyed by another Vanuatu official last week.

PNG parliament resumes Bougainville independence debate, vote timing unclear

Debate on Bougainville's political future resumed in Papua New Guinea's parliament, with no fixed timing for a vote on ratifying the 2019 referendum result the government tabled in August. Minister for Bougainville Affairs Manasseh Makiba said MPs would be given time to speak and a vote could come at the end of the current session. "The referendum result is a constitutional issue that members will finally decide on," Makiba said.
