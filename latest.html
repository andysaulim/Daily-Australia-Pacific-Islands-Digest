US-China-Pacific

US adds $450 million to Palau, FSM and Marshall Islands compact funding

The United States will provide more than $450 million in new funding to Palau, the Federated States of Micronesia and the Marshall Islands for the 2027 financial year under updated Compact of Free Association agreements. Palau receives over $41 million, the FSM $140 million, and the Marshall Islands more than $171 million, plus a final $100 million contribution to its Compact Trust Fund. The agreements grant Washington exclusive defence rights in the three states, part of a 20-year, $7.1 billion package. The funding follows annual meetings in September between US officials and the three governments.

Quad leaders announce defence and critical-minerals deals in Manila

Anthony Albanese sat down with Donald Trump, Japan's Sanae Takaichi and India's Narendra Modi for the first Quad leaders' meeting Trump has attended in person. The group announced new joint military ventures after discussions on artificial intelligence and critical minerals, with Trump calling the grouping 'very important' and telling the leaders 'my phone is always open.' Trump said the minerals deals 'guarantee safety.' Albanese then flew to Shenzhen for APEC.

Australia sanctions Israeli settler leader Daniella Weiss

Australia sanctioned three individuals and an organisation tied to Israeli settlement expansion in the West Bank, among them Daniella Weiss, described by the ABC as the godmother of the settler movement, and Nachala, the organisation she leads.
