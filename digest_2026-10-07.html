AUKUS

Trump announces multibillion-dollar shipyard for AUKUS submarines

Donald Trump travelled to Baltimore to announce a new United States shipyard costing billions of dollars, which administration officials said would bolster the lagging production rate of Virginia-class vessels. Production tempo is the central variable in the AUKUS submarine pipeline: Australia's acquisition pathway depends on American yards building faster than they currently do. The announcement carried no figure for Australian participation and no revised delivery schedule.

Pacific-Diplomacy

PNG commits US$5.5 million to Tuvalu coastal reclamation

Papua New Guinea will give Tuvalu about US$5.5 million (K25 million) over five years for coastal reclamation, Prime Minister James Marape said at Pre-COP31 in Funafuti, with funds allocated from each annual budget. "It would buy them two or three more generations of life," Marape said, urging others to "rally behind them" rather than attend COPs "with no serious commitments." Tuvaluan Prime Minister Feleti Teo said the money would secure the country's most exposed coastline. Tuvalu's atolls total 26 square kilometres.

Trade-Economy

High Court puts overseas coal emissions into Australian planning law

A majority of the High Court found planning authorities must consider how emissions burned overseas are managed when approving fossil fuel projects in Australia. The ruling favours a Hunter Valley residents group that halted the Mount Pleasant coal mine expansion in the New South Wales Court of Appeal last year. Bloomberg reports the decision tests a $49 billion coal export sector; New South Wales mines will now have to account for downstream emissions at approval stage.

per Bloomberg: "Climate Ruling Deals Blow to Australia's $49 Billion Coal Export Sector" · also The Australian

Labor defends $20 million bill for Pacific climate diplomacy

Pre-COP31 in Fiji and Tuvalu was designed to showcase Australian leadership before COP31, but major world leaders skipped it, leaving Anthony Albanese's government defending a $20 million cost, SMH reports. The domestic argument lands while Canberra weighs several fossil fuel project expansions at home. Australia's Pacific climate credentials are the hinge of its COP31 partnership with the region.
