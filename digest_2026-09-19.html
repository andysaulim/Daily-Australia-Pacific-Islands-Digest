Diplomats say Australia withdrew from West Bank sanctions coalition at the 11th hour

European diplomats said they were surprised and disappointed when Australia declined to join a 12-country coalition moving to sanction trade with Israeli settlements in the West Bank. The signatories were the UK, France, Canada, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Ireland, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Spain, and Sweden. The UK-led statement declared that ethnic cleansing was taking place in the West Bank and that Israel's occupation was unlawful. Canberra took part in the talks but withdrew late, and the government said it was "working closely together towards the same objective" with international partners.

Fiji declares national HIV emergency after twelve-fold rise in diagnoses

Fiji's health ministry declared a national HIV emergency after recording 2,016 new diagnoses in 2025, a twelve-fold increase since 2010, with an estimated 9,100 Fijians now living with the virus, one in every 60 adults. Only 39 percent know their status and just 22 percent are accessing antiretroviral treatment, according to UNAIDS. Health Minister Antonio Lalabalavu said the current response level "is no longer sufficient" and called for coordinated action with UNAIDS and the World Health Organization.
