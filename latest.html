Trump vows 'tremendous tax' on US companies building data centres offshore

Donald Trump said he would impose a "tremendous tax" on American companies that build data centres outside the United States. "We're not allowing data centres to go into other countries," he told a White House briefing, framing the move as part of the artificial intelligence contest with China. Microsoft announced a $25 billion Australian data centre investment in April and Amazon a $20 billion plan in June. Trump also talked down the Quad artificial intelligence agreement with Australia, India, and Japan: "It's wonderful to say we're working with other countries to build, but I want it in this country."
