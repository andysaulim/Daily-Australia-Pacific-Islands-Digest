Fiji restores Great Council of Chiefs as standalone institution

Fiji's Parliament passed the Bose Levu Vakaturaga Bill 2026 on Thursday, separating the chiefs' council from the Ministry of iTaukei Affairs. Minister Ifereimi Vasu said the institution's earlier removal had created a gap between the chiefs, the vanua and his ministry: "They were operating under the ministry, but now they stand alone." BLV chairman Ratu Viliame Seruvakula said recognition "is not an end in itself. It is a responsibility to serve."

Fiji seeks Australian uncrewed craft to extend maritime surveillance

Fiji hopes to receive uncrewed surface craft from Australia capable of monitoring up to 40 nautical miles out to sea in any weather, Defence and Veteran Affairs Minister Pio Tikoduadua said. The craft would give the Republic of Fiji Military Forces all-weather maritime domain awareness. The disclosure comes as Fiji hosts Pre-COP31 delegations in Nadi and Tuvalu this week.
