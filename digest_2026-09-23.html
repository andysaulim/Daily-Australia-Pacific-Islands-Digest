Pacific-Politics

Fiji review recommends scrapping coup immunity, bicameral parliament

Fiji's Constitution Review Commission has recommended repealing the "absolute and irrevocable immunity" shielding participants in the 1987, 2000 and 2006 coups from prosecution, a change that would touch Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka, who led the 1987 coup. The 236-page report, tabled at a special sitting in Suva on Tuesday, also proposes restoring a bicameral parliament of sixty seats, constitutional recognition for the Great Council of Chiefs, and refocusing the military on external defence. Cabinet says tabling does not equal adoption.

US embassy warns Australia's algorithm law risks censorship

The U.S. embassy in Canberra issued a formal response warning that Australia's draft law letting users opt out of systems that choose what appears in their feeds could amount to censorship of legitimate speech, an unusual direct intervention in Australian domestic regulation. The warning lands as Anthony Albanese presses a "digital duty of care" framework at the UN General Assembly, and as Donald Trump rejected what he called a "globalist scheme" to regulate AI from the same podium.

AFR: RBA on track for two rate rises before Christmas

The Reserve Bank is expected to raise the cash rate on September 29, 2026 and again in November, the Australian Financial Review reports, reversing the two consecutive holds at 4.35 percent recorded through August.
