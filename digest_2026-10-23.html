Pacific-Politics

PNG parliament opens debate on ratifying Bougainville independence

Papua New Guinea's national parliament opened debate this week on whether to ratify the result of Bougainville's 2019 independence referendum, in which 97.7 percent of voters backed secession. Every member is being given a chance to speak before the debate resumes in November and moves toward a ratification vote. The government says that while ordinary constitutional amendments need a two-thirds majority, ratifying Bougainville's independence would require support from three-quarters of MPs, according to ABC's Tok Pisin-language Wantok program.

Solomon Islands confiscates Chinese cargo ship after law-breaking voyage

The Solomon Islands government has taken ownership of the Chinese-flagged cargo vessel MV Wealth after it breached maritime law entering the country's waters in June, according to ABC's Tok Pisin-language Wantok program. A Solomon Islands patrol boat arrested the ship in an operation involving the Forum Fisheries Agency and other government agencies. Roughly twenty Asian nationals aboard appeared in court this week and remain on bail in Rove ahead of a further hearing next week.

Pacific-Diplomacy

US launches Pacific police network to counter drug cartels, hub in Palau

The US State Department has launched a Pacific police program designating Palau as the regional hub for training and operations against narcotics trafficking, backed by $44 million in new programming. Officials said Pacific-transiting narcotics from South American and Southeast Asian cartels have surpassed the historic flow from Asia since 2024. The announcement followed the inaugural US-Pacific Islands Conference of Police Ministers, held in Los Angeles on October 21 and 22.
