Albanese calls for a US-China AI deal as Trump moves against regulation

Anthony Albanese urged the United States and China to strike a deal on artificial intelligence "in the interests of humanity" during a visit to Apple's headquarters in Cupertino, California, en route to New York. He said global rules were needed "because it is moving very quickly, and we need to make sure that humans remain in control", and that he would raise the issue with like-minded leaders at next week's United Nations General Assembly. The prime minister also left the door open to changing Australian copyright law to attract AI investment. Donald Trump said he will form an AI force and appoint an AI adviser while warning he will not hinder the industry's growth.

AU-Politics

Coalition pledges to halve fuel excise when Brent averages $US100

Angus Taylor announced a "Fuel Price Shield" that would halve the fuel excise whenever Brent crude averages $US100 a barrel over a two-week period, cutting about $15 from the average tank. The Coalition says the measure would be fully funded by $8 billion generated from its planned cuts to the tobacco excise. Taylor tied the policy to a re-escalation of war in the Middle East pushing pump prices toward $3 a litre. Jim Chalmers dismissed the policy and said the government was not considering future cuts to the fuel excise.
