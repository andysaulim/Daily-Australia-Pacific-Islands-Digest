PNG Supreme Court orders release of Solwara deep sea mining documents
Opponents of the Solwara deep sea mining project won a further ruling requiring Papua New Guinea's government to release project documents. The Centre for Environmental Law and Community Rights sued the Mineral Resources Authority and the Conservation and Environtment Protection Authority in 2019; a 2024 Supreme Court order to hand over the mining licence and environmental permit has still not been met by the Mineral Resources Authority. NZ-Foreign-Policy · RNZ Pacific
Treasury says Luxon and Peters committed New Zealand to hosting the 2027 Forum without Cabinet approval
Christopher Luxon and Winston Peters did not consult Cabinet before committing New Zealand to host the Pacific Islands Forum in 2027, the Treasury said. Budget 2026 committed $20 million across this year and next for the summit, with Peters making the offer at a Forum Foreign Ministers meeting in August of last year and the official announcement following in September. March advice to Finance Minister Nicola Willis recommended excluding a NZ$30m funding proposal, said "Cabinet approval was not sought before announcement of the hosting arrangement", and that "the value for money of the bid is not clear"; Willis was separately advised to cut the package to $10m, closer to the cost of hosting in 2011, since "$6.4m for policy work" was discretionary or scalable.
