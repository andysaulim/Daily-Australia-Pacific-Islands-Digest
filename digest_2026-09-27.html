Forum leaders reaffirm UN mandate in Guterres' final Pacific dialogue

Pacific Islands Forum leaders met UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres on the margins of the 81st UN General Assembly, in what the Forum described as his final dialogue with leaders in this format before his term concludes at the end of 2026. Leaders carried forward the mandate agreed at the 55th Leaders Meeting in Palau, pressing climate and development finance, the Pacific Resilience Facility, and the Blue Pacific as an Ocean of Peace, while raising sea-level rise, nuclear issues and transnational crime. Forum chair and Palau President Surangel Whipps Jr. said the Pacific "must continue to navigate together."

China's sixth Antarctic base plan stirs Australian defence concern

China plans to build its sixth Antarctic research station next year in Marie Byrd Land, a remote and little-studied stretch of the continent's west. Independent senator Jacqui Lambie said "five bases have already been built, and China claims there is no geopolitical motive." Australia operates three permanent year-round bases and claims 42 percent of the continent; Russia has five. Jiliang Chen, a lecturer in Antarctic law and governance at the University of Tasmania, frames the station less as a military asset than as a marker of influence inside Antarctic governance institutions.
