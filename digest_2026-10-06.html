AU-Defense

Hundreds protest against Australia's largest defence expo in Perth

About four hundred protesters gathered in Perth against the Land Forces expo, Australia's largest land defence event, held October 6 to 8 at the Perth Convention and Exhibition Centre with industry representatives from sixty countries and almost one thousand exhibiting organisations. Western Australia police received ten days of temporary special powers and $11.5 million for policing, including the ability to ban excluded persons from the security zone. Placards opposed the AUKUS submarine deal, two years after clashes at the expo's Melbourne edition.

AU-Politics

Coalition unveils 100,000 migration cap in sweeping overhaul

Angus Taylor unveiled a Coalition plan to cut net overseas migration to 100,000 for two years, then 130,000 and 160,000 in years three and four, slashing the temporary intake by about 650,000 over four years through changes to graduate, humanitarian and bridging visas. Net overseas migration fell to 292,100 in the year to March; Labor is targeting 245,000 this financial year and 225,000 the next. Taylor offered no modelling of the economic impact, promising costings closer to the 2028 election.

per ABC Politics: "Graduate visas face the axe in Coalition migration overhaul" · also SMH Federal Politics: "Taylor enters three-way migration battle with 100,000 target as Labor and One Nation fire back"
Pacific-Diplomacy

Albanese warns Pacific leaders of a hellish Australian summer

Anthony Albanese told Pacific leaders gathered in Fiji for Pre-COP31 that climate change combined with a monster El Nino would hit Australia hard with fire, cyclones and extreme heat. The four-day meeting, co-hosted by Fiji and Tuvalu, sets the agenda ahead of November's COP31 in Türkiye. Papua New Guinea Prime Minister James Marape, the first leader to arrive, pledged financial help for Tuvalu's land reclamation project and attacked countries that keep emitting without addressing Pacific damage.

per SMH: "Fire, cyclones, extreme heat: Albanese's hellish summer forecast" · also ABC Pacific: "PNG PM pledges financial help to Tuvalu to combat rising seas at Pre-COP"
AU-Foreign-Policy

Greens under fire over opposition to Ukraine military aid

Ukraine's top representative in Australia urged the Greens to reconsider their opposition to providing military aid against Russia's invasion, as Labor and the Coalition accused the party of a naive stance that would hand Vladimir Putin a gift. The Ukrainian diaspora joined both major parties in the criticism. The row follows the Greens setting defence red lines opposing arms for Ukraine and seeking to curtail the US alliance, carried in this brief on October 3.
