Solomon Islands PM Wale flies home from Palau as cabinet turns against him

Solomon Islands Prime Minister Matthew Wale was forced back home within hours of landing in Palau after Home Affairs Minister Manasseh Maelanga moved a no-confidence motion; Maelanga and two other cabinet ministers have resigned from Wale's GREAT coalition. New Zealand Foreign Minister Winston Peters said Palau's President Surangel Whipps Jr personally flew Wale home, calling the timing unfortunate.

So what: Leaves Wale, the outgoing Forum chair, absent for the handover of the chairmanship to Palau's Whipps Jr, with further cabinet resignations, possibly including Finance Minister Gordon Darcy Lilo, still rumored.

US-China-Pacific

New Zealand, US to fund Cook Islands port upgrade on former US military site

The United States and New Zealand will co-fund a port upgrade on Penrhyn atoll, a former US military site in the northern Cook Islands, announced by Cook Islands PM Mark Brown, NZ Foreign Minister Winston Peters and US Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau from Palau. The State Department will contribute $50 million, working with Congress, with New Zealand adding NZ$17.5 million, about $10 million.

So what: Adds a concrete US-New Zealand infrastructure commitment in the Cook Islands, announced alongside the critical minerals framing carried by RNZ around the deal.

per Reuters: "New Zealand, US to fund Cook Islands port upgrade" · also Beehive (NZ Govt)
