Vanuatu ferry sinking leaves up to 30 missing, exposes warning gap

The inter-island ferry MV Matui sank in rough seas between Santo and Ambae on Friday, with two deaths confirmed, 15 rescued and up to 30 people still missing. Vanuatu Meteorology and Geo-Hazards Department director Levu Antfalo said warnings went out on Tuesday and a marine strong wind warning at 5am Friday forecast seas of up to three metres, but that stopping vessels "is not our mandate". Pilot Mark Turnbull, who flew the first aerial search, said help took more than 30 hours to arrive.
