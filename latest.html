AU-Defense

ADF recruitment climbs as full-time force hits 25-year high

The Australian Defence Force has grown to more than 64,000 full-time personnel across the navy, army and air force this year, its largest size in 25 years, the Guardian reports. The growth comes as perceptions of regional conflict risk rise. Recruiters are pitching the services directly to prospective enlistees, including at a session in Parramatta in Sydney's west led by army corporal Joel Kellner. Recruitment and retention shortfalls have constrained Australian force structure planning for most of the past decade.

Parliament resumes for first sitting week since Haines attack

Members of Parliament returned to Canberra on Sunday for the first sitting week since independent MP Helen Haines lost her legs in an alleged attack outside her electorate office in Wangaratta, SMH reports. Solomon Islands PM presses Australian employers to lift PALM intake to 5,000 a year

Solomon Islands Prime Minister Matthew Wale challenged employers approved under Australia's Pacific Australia Labour Mobility scheme to help recruit 5,000 Solomon Islanders annually for work in Australia, PNG Post-Courier reports. Labour mobility is the largest single channel of Australian economic engagement with Solomon Islands, whose prime minister currently chairs the Pacific Islands Forum.
