AU-Foreign-Policy

Burke reworks backpacker crackdown while targeting UK visa stream

Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke all but confirmed the government is returning to the drawing board on its working holiday visa overhaul, saying Australia must "find a way" to reduce the number of British backpackers arriving under the free trade deal introduced in 2023, which allows UK citizens to stay up to three years without work requirements. "We can find a way to bring that back to something more sensible and in the national interest, without jeopardising the other parts of the free trade agreement," he said. In August Anthony Albanese ruled out imposing regional work requirements on UK backpackers.
