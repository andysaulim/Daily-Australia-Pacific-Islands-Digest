Pacific-Politics

Rabuka delays Constitution Bill vote after mounting pressure

Sitiveni Rabuka told parliament on Monday that the Special Parliamentary Committee on the Constitution (Amendment) Bill 2026 would "table its review report at a later sitting date", reversing his confirmation a day earlier that the bill would be tabled this week. He gave no new date, saying the extension would let the committee "listen to more submissions from the public" and give parliamentary staff time to compile the final report. Rabuka has tied the absence of an announced election date to the reform process running in parallel; the hard deadline for the election is February 6, 2027.
