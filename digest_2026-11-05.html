Pacific-Diplomacy

Fijian committee recommends deferring ratification of the Vuvale Union and Ocean of Peace Alliance

Fiji's parliamentary Standing Committee on Foreign Affairs and Defence told legislators on Wednesday that ratification of the two agreements signed with Australia in July should be deferred, with its chairperson Lenora Qereqeretabua, who is also deputy speaker and assistant minister of foreign affairs, delivering the recommendation. Anthony Albanese had described the treaty as "one of the most significant endeavours" undertaken by Canberra. Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka said he was "very disappointed" and would pursue "another route" to get the agreements ratified. Traditional leader Ratu Tevita Mara had urged lawmakers in September to vote both down.

Trade-Economy

RBA deputy governor concedes public trust in the inflation fight is slipping

The Reserve Bank's Monetary Policy Board lifted the cash rate to 4.85 percent this week, up from 4.6 percent and the highest level in 18 years, in a majority decision. Deputy governor Andrew Hauser said a bank survey showed trust in its near-term ability to meet its monetary policy objectives had "fallen materially", while overall trust remained "high and stable", adding that "consumer confidence is exceptionally weak". The AFR reports board members privately rebuked Hauser for signalling a fortnight before the meeting that rates were very likely to rise.
