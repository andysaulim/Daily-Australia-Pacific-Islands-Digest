Albanese tells Putin to end Ukraine war 'now' as Trump, Xi and Putin meet

Anthony Albanese called on Vladimir Putin to end his invasion of Ukraine "now" in an address to APEC leaders in Shenzhen, hours before Putin sat down with Donald Trump and Xi Jinping for the first trilateral meeting between the three powers. The leaders talked for about 90 minutes at Kylin Villa, posing in front of their flags without shaking hands. Trump confirmed Ukraine was on the agenda alongside trade and nuclear arms control; the New Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty lapsed in February.

Unemployment hits 4.9 percent, the highest since 2021

Australia's jobless rate rose 0.2 percentage points in October to 4.9 percent, the highest since 2021, against market expectations of no change. Back-to-back monthly job losses undercut the Reserve Bank's argument that the labour market remains tight, and the three members who dissented from the last decision now look vindicated. The AFR reports bond traders pricing a small chance of a cut as soon as February. Angus Taylor blamed government "overspending".

Canberra to set seven-year prison term for photographing defence sites

Australia will criminalise photographing designated defence sites with penalties of up to seven years in prison, Politico reports, as part of a broader expansion of defence powers that also covers immunity arrangements for United States troops in Australia.
