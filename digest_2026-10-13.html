Five Eyes nears deal on shared military AI backbone

The New Zealand Defence Force says Five Eyes partners are finalising arrangements for Arcadia, a shared digital backbone that embeds AI across New Zealand, Australian, Canadian, British and American militaries. Fiji justice minister resigns after explicit video scandal

Fiji Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka confirmed Tuesday he has accepted the resignation of Justice Minister Siromi Turaga, days after an explicit video appeared on Turaga's Facebook profile on the eve of Fiji's independence anniversary. Turaga, also acting Attorney-General, was in New Zealand when the clip surfaced; no replacement will be named immediately. It is the second such scandal in Rabuka's cabinet, after Information Minister Lynda Tabuya's video leaked in 2024.

Panguna mine reopening push revives Bougainville civil war fears

Rising global copper demand has fueled interest in reopening Bougainville's Panguna mine, closed for four decades after fueling a civil war that killed thousands. The New York Times reports concern that a rush to restart the mine, seen as a potential funding source for a future Bougainville nation, risks a return to violence. The mine sits at the center of the autonomous Papua New Guinea region's path toward independence.
