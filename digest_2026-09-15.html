US-China-Pacific

US think tank urges shift of American forces to Guam and the second island chain

Defense Priorities proposes moving much of the US military presence away from the first island chain, including Japan, Taiwan, South Korea and the Philippines, and concentrating forces further from China's coast. Its explainer, "A second island chain strategy for Asia", identifies Guam, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, Palau, the Federated States of Micronesia and the Marshall Islands as candidate locations. Researcher Jennifer Kavanagh said the United States already has at least 8,000 personnel along the second island chain, most on Guam, and proposes dispersing rather than enlarging that force, with Tinian among smaller air bases hosting intelligence, air defences and drones.

Pacific-Politics

Armed attack on Indian miner at Bougainville's Panguna mine kills one

Armed men entered the Panguna copper mine site on Sunday and set fire to machinery belonging to Lloyds Metals & Energy Ltd, whose staff were also attacked. Bougainville police chief Francis Tokura said a group from Buin in southern Bougainville invaded the site with high-powered weapons; at least one man died in the shootout that followed and police have arrested up to several suspects. PNG Trade Minister Richard Maru said he had repeatedly advised the company against investing at Panguna, while Autonomous Bougainville Government President Ishmael Toroama called the attack "terrorism".

Canberra weighs EU associate status after Brussels warms to Canada

Brussels has responded positively to the prospect of Canada becoming its first "associate" member of the European Union, and one Australian minister is open to Australia following, the Sydney Morning Herald reported. Reuters, reporting the same day, quoted a minister saying Australia is "on the same page" as Canada as it seeks a deeper alliance with the EU. Neither government has set out what associate status would entail.
