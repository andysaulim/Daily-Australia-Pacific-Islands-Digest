RBA lifts cash rate to 4.85 percent, an 18-year high

The Reserve Bank raised its cash rate by 25 basis points to 4.85 percent, the fifth rise this year and the highest level in eighteen years, with board members splitting six to three in favour. The bank said inflation expectations had become entrenched, that inflation would stay above 3 percent for the coming year, and that it would not return to the target band until late 2027. Governor Michele Bullock said the economy was not in recession but conceded it "sure feels like one". Treasurer Jim Chalmers flagged budget cuts to help bring inflation down.

Pacific-Politics

Fiji military commander opposes coup immunity as constitution bill stalls

The Republic of Fiji Military Forces told a special parliamentary committee it opposes new immunity provisions for coup-makers in the government's Constitution (Amendment) Bill. Commander Kalouniwai said the RFMF did not appear with "clean hands" after the overthrows of 1987, 2000, and 2006, and described an "architecture of rebellion" resting on secrecy, governance through fear, misplaced loyalty, and constitutional immunity. Prime Minister Sitiveni Rabuka, who led the 1987 coup, told the committee the country relies on the military during "disruptions", and has delayed the bill's final reading.
