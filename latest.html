China-Pacific

Taiwan completes aid and LNG cutoff to Papua New Guinea

Taiwan's Foreign Ministry confirmed it has closed its Port Moresby office, withdrawn all stationed personnel, and terminated "all technical cooperation and aid programs in Papua New Guinea". Taipei said Port Moresby had "renewed its unilateral demands" for the Taipei Economic Office's staff to leave within a specified timeframe. The decision follows the July announcement by PNG Foreign Minister Justin Tkatchenko that Prime Minister James Marape's government would honour the one-China policy, and extends to a halt on Taiwanese purchases of PNG liquefied natural gas.

New Zealand coalition's majority rests on 399,000 uncounted votes

Preliminary results give Christopher Luxon's coalition 67 of 131 seats, one above the 66 needed to govern, after a record 11-seat overhang inflated Parliament. National's party vote entitles it to 35 MPs, but leads in 42 electorates add seven overhang seats, while Te Pati Maori's five electorate wins against a one-seat party vote add four more. An estimated 399,000 special votes, roughly one in seven cast, remain uncounted and have historically favoured the left.

One Nation draws level with Labor in new national poll

Roy Morgan called the federal contest "too close to call", with Labor on a primary vote of 25.5 percent, One Nation on 24.5 percent, and the Liberal-National Coalition third on 22 percent.
