AU-Politics

Canberra creates AI safety regulator, mandates breach reporting after Medicare hack

Anthony Albanese released the recommendations of a rapid review into the June breach of a Medicare statistics reporting portal administered by Services Australia, and the government accepted all of them. They include mandatory reporting of rogue AI incidents, fines for companies that fail to report, and independent safety assessments of AI models, enforced by a new regulator setting mandatory safety and security standards for developers. OpenAI will not face criminal penalties over the breach, though Albanese flagged changes to Australia's criminal law for future cases. The review's findings and the report itself were not released.

Pacific-Diplomacy

COP31 opens in Antalya with Australia as lead negotiator and the Pacific pressing its case

Two weeks of climate talks began in Antalya, Türkiye, running to November 20, with Australia in the lead negotiator's role. The Pacific hosted the Pre-COP talks last month and delegations want regional interests at the centre of the formal negotiations.
