US-China-Pacific

Fiji demands US evidence behind China-linked bribery sanctions

The US State Department sanctioned Zhao Fugang, director of the Overseas Chinese Service Center in Fiji, alleging he "abused his public position by paying bribes to Fijian citizens to advance China-based government, business and criminal interests." Zhao, a Chinese-born naturalised Fijian citizen who runs property and tourism businesses in Suva and owns the Yue Lai Hotel, is banned from the United States along with his immediate family. Fiji pledged cooperation against transnational crime but asked Washington to substantiate the allegations; the US embassy in Suva separately warned on Facebook of China-linked operators infiltrating local governments, a pattern security experts call "systemic geocriminality."

NZ-Defense

NZDF joined Five Eyes digital backbone after internal cutoff warning

Newly released briefings show a New Zealand Defence Force commander warned in April that without committing to Project Arcadia the NZDF "will not have access to these foundational capabilities, becoming effectively cut off from future FVEY information exchanges." The United States announced the build of the digital backbone linking the five militaries in May; New Zealand signed up the same month. The papers said participation would let the NZDF go "far beyond" what it could afford alone, "without loss of national control," and that Defence would seek additional funding in Budget 2027.

Pacific-Diplomacy

Pacific Resilience Facility pledges reach US$190 million before COP31

Pacific Islands Forum Secretary General Baron Divavesi Waqa welcomed US$13.3 million in new commitments to the Pacific Resilience Facility, taking total pledges to US$190 million and following Fiji's earlier US$10 million contribution. The pledges landed as Pre-COP31 wrapped up in Nadi, with Australia announcing a separate $3 million for a region-led climate facility. Forum members are pressing major emitters for faster emissions cuts and easier access to climate finance ahead of COP31 in Turkiye next month.
