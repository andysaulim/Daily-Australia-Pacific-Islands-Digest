Solomon Islands Opposition alleges Australian interference as treaty deal lands

Solomon Islands' Opposition says a leaked message between the Australian High Commission and Deputy Prime Minister Francis Sade appeared to show Australian funding being used to influence the outcome of a no-confidence motion against the prime minister. Sade rejected the claim, saying the text was an update on successful treaty negotiations and should not be used as political ammunition. The motion was withdrawn just as negotiations wrapped up, with Australia agreeing in principle to a billion-dollar package. Lowy Institute research fellow Oliver Nobetau said the common thread across recent bilateral agreements is language on denying strategic access to third parties.

Canberra releases NZYQ deportation agreement with Naoero

The Australian government released the agreement to deport more than 350 refugees and asylum seekers, the "NZYQ cohort", to Naoero, formerly Nauru. Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke said in August 2025 that the deal gave Naoero AU$2.5 billion over thirty years. Green Party senator David Shoebridge said the release only scratches the surface, that the government "has been far from forthright", and that it continues to resist producing a separate memorandum establishing the financial arrangement.
