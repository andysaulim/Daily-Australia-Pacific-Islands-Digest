New Zealand election leaves National and Labour locked in a historic deadlock

New Zealand's election produced one of the tightest results in MMP history: National is projected on 42 seats to Labour's 36, the two parties separated by 3,650 party votes, 710,330 for Labour against 706,680 for National. Christopher Luxon told supporters at Auckland's Viaduct Events Centre the result was "too close to call" but that National had "a pathway to forming the next government". AU-Defense

Albanese scraps Victoria Barracks sale after cross-party backlash

Anthony Albanese has abandoned the sale of Sydney's 166-year-old Victoria Barracks, along with Victoria Barracks Melbourne, Fort Queenscliff and Point Cook (RAAF Williams), transferring the four heritage-listed sites from Defence to the Finance Department. They were among 67 Defence properties marked for sale after an independent estate audit in February, intended to raise $1.8 billion. Richard Marles denied the decision would undermine Australia's defence. The Coalition, Greens, One Nation, teal independents, RSL Australia and former prime minister John Howard had all condemned the sale.
