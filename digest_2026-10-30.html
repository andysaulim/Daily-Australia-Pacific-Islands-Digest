Pacific-Diplomacy

Australia hands Fiji AI sea drones to close a maritime surveillance gap

Australia is giving Fiji uncrewed Bluebottle surface drones, Australian-built 7.4-metre craft that can remain at sea for up to six months and use artificial intelligence to flag suspicious activity. Fiji's Defence Minister Pio Tikoduadua said the country's waters are too vast to monitor: "We don't even have eyes on it, let alone have the capability to affect anything on it." Fiji's navy operates just two large patrol boats, both Australian-donated Guardian-class vessels.

Pacific-Politics

Former Kiribati president calls PIF-Israel partnership indefensible

Anote Tong, former president of Kiribati and chair of the Pacific Elders' Voice, said the argument that religious affinity justifies the Pacific Islands Forum's decision to make Israel a sectoral partner "does not stack up." The group, made up of former prime ministers, presidents, diplomats and academics, says the move conflicts with the region's commitments to human rights, self-determination and international law, citing a Middle East fact-finding report by the Elders Group that included former New Zealand prime minister Helen Clark.
