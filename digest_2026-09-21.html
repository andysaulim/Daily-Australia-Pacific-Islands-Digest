Peters says nuclear-free policy 'costs did occur' in final speech as foreign minister

AU-Defense

Australia and New Zealand sign Plan Tasman naval pact

The two navies signed Plan Tasman, covering personnel, training, development, warfighting, engineering, regulation and international engagement. Royal Australian Navy Deputy Chief Rear Admiral Darren Grogan said it "provides a practical framework to strengthen the way we train, operate and develop capability together." Royal New Zealand Navy Deputy Chief Commodore Karl Woodhead said the strategic guidance is "to deepen and formalise the alliance," describing the plan as "a shift from a history of cooperation based on tradition to integration by design" in a maritime relationship more than 160 years old.

per RNZ: "New Zealand and Australia deepen naval partnership" · also Reuters
AU-Foreign-Policy

Albanese launches Australia's bid for UN Security Council seat

Anthony Albanese launched Australia's campaign for a two-year non-permanent seat on the UN Security Council, saying Australia is "in a pretty strong position" with Finland the only other nominee for two available spots. The secret ballot falls in mid-2028 and requires a two-thirds majority of UN General Assembly members. Albanese cited Australia's social media restrictions for minors as evidence of middle-power weight and named former News Corp executive Siobhan McKenna as the next consul-general in New York.
