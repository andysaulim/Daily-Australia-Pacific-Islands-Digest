Rio Tinto pledges $370 million for Panguna mine legacy in Bougainville

Rio Tinto will contribute $370 million over coming years to a new fund addressing the toxic legacy of its Panguna mine, nearly forty years after local opposition forced the 1989 closure that triggered a civil war killing an estimated 10,000 to 15,000 people. Chief safety and technical officer Mark Davies called the pledge an important milestone following an impact assessment that found mine waste and decaying infrastructure still affect thousands. Locals cited clean water and transport as immediate priorities.
