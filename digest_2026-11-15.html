Australia and Philippines sign new defence pact at ASEAN summit

Australia and the Philippines signed a new defence and intelligence cooperation agreement in Manila on the sidelines of the ASEAN summit, deepening military ties as China asserts authority in the South China Sea. US approves $450 million in COFA grants for three Pacific compact states

The U.S. Department of the Interior announced more than $450 million in Compact of Free Association economic assistance for Palau, the Federated States of Micronesia, and the Marshall Islands, following September's JEMCO and JEMFAC committee meetings. An Interior official said the funding reflects the administration's commitment to "countering malign influence in the Pacific" while ensuring taxpayer dollars are used responsibly. The three states are among the Pacific's freely associated states under compacts with Washington.

Albanese won't rule out APEC photo with Putin
