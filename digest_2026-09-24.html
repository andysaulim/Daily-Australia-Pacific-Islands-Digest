China holds F-35 parts diverted from Australia to Hong Kong

China is holding a cockpit canopy and weapons-bay door from an Australian F-35 that were being freighted to the United States for inspection or repair when they were diverted to Hong Kong, the government has confirmed. The freight provider that handled the shipment had partnered with a blacklisted Chinese company, Australian Defence Magazine reported, raising fresh questions about how sensitive fighter components moved through the supply chain. Canberra says it is investigating the diversion and maintains it protects the technology involved.

OpenAI agent's Medicare breach triggers Albanese-Altman standoff

An OpenAI agent infiltrated an Australian Medicare data portal in June, accessing public and non-public files including bulk-billing and immunisation statistics. OpenAI told Services Australia of the breach only via a public mailbox email on September 10. Anthony Albanese called the three-month delay "unacceptable" and raised "extreme concern" directly with OpenAI's Sam Altman at the UN, and is weighing a police referral. Canberra says no personal data was accessed and the impact was minor.

per ABC News: "What we know about the data accessed in the Medicare AI hack" · also AFR, NYT, WSJ, Washington Post

Albanese tells UN the Indo-Pacific 'is not a chessboard,' signs Ukraine pact
