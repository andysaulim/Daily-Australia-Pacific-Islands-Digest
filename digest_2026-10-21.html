Israel joins Pacific Islands Forum as development partner

Israel has been accepted as a partner of the Pacific Islands Forum, the Israeli Foreign Ministry announced Wednesday, with the decision taken at last month's leaders meeting in Palau. As a partner, Israel joins the Forum's Sectoral Partnership Stream, focused on water management, desalination, agriculture, and climate adaptation; the PIF now counts more than two dozen external dialogue partners, including the United States, China, the United Kingdom, France, and the European Union.
