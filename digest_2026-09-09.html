Solomon Islands Opposition accuses Australia of foreign interference

Solomon Islands' Opposition said communication between the Australian High Commission and Deputy Prime Minister Francis Sade was "deeply concerning" and appeared to show Australian funding being used to influence the outcome of a no-confidence motion against Prime Minister Matthew Wale. Sade rejected the claim, saying the text was an update on successful treaty negotiations. The motion was withdrawn as talks wrapped up, with Australia agreeing in principle to a billion-dollar package. Lowy Institute research fellow Oliver Nobetau said recent bilateral agreements share language on denying strategic access to third parties.

Wong criticised by former colleague for skipping Israeli settlement trade ban

A former Labor colleague called Foreign Minister Penny Wong "cowardly" for declining to join the British-led crackdown on imports from Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, while Israel's ambassador applauded Canberra for standing aside. Pacific-Diplomacy

Australia releases NZYQ deportation agreement with Naoero

The Australian government published the agreement to deport more than 350 refugees and asylum seekers, the "NZYQ cohort", to Naoero, formerly Nauru. Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke said in August 2025 that the deal gave Naoero AU$2.5 billion over thirty years. Greens senator David Shoebridge said the release only scratches the surface and that the government continues to resist producing a separate memorandum setting up the financial arrangement. Australia turned to Naoero after the High Court's late-2023 NZYQ ruling.

Northern Ambae communities in water crisis after volcanic ashfall
Communities in northern Ambae are without clean drinking water after renewed eruption of the Manaro Voui volcano covered existing water sources in ash following months of dry weather. Chief Antoine Vuti of Nangire Village told reporter Leah Lowonbu that residents are trucking drinking water from other parts of the island, and that ash in the water is driving persistent coughing, particularly among children.
