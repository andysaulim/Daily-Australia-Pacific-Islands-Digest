Hanson calls Taylor a "smarmy management consultant" as CPAC crowd heckles Liberal leader

Pauline Hanson told the Conservative Political Action Conference in Brisbane that attendees should not trust the Coalition, urging them to put the Liberals or Nationals second behind One Nation on their ballots. Angus Taylor, billed as keynote speaker and heckled from the floor, said the Coalition "got some things wrong" in signing up to net zero and increasing immigration, and framed the next election as a "spiritual fight" for the soul of the nation. It was the first address to the conference by a sitting Liberal leader.

Bowen rules out Russian fuel after Trump strikes diesel deal with Putin

Energy and climate change minister Chris Bowen said Australia did not "need or want Russian fuel" and that Russia "should not be rewarded for their illegal and immoral invasion of Ukraine", speaking as he updated reporters on Australian fuel reserves. Donald Trump publicly thanked Vladimir Putin for the deal. Brent crude settled at 104.72, up 4.39 percent on October 9.
