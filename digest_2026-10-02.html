Fiji creates Counter Narcotics Bureau after cocaine scandal

Fiji's parliament passed legislation creating a Counter Narcotics Bureau with new powers to search, seize and run covert operations, one of eight bills passed in a sitting that began on October 1, 2026, and ended at 1:30 a.m. the next day. The law follows the disappearance of fifty-two kilograms of cocaine from Suva High Court evidence, including thirty-two kilograms swapped for flour. Political analyst Jope Tarai said a freer media and "directed political will" have pushed the issue into the open.

Navy's two largest warships sidelined by propulsion failures

HMAS Canberra and HMAS Adelaide, the Royal Australian Navy's largest warships, have both been sidelined by propulsion failures, The Australian reports. The simultaneous outage removes Australia's primary amphibious lift capacity from service at a time when the 2026 National Defence Strategy places weight on littoral manoeuvre and regional disaster response.
