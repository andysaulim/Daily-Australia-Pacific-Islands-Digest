RBA lifts cash rate to 4.6 percent, highest since 2011

The Reserve Bank of Australia raised the cash rate by 0.25 percentage points to 4.6 percent, its fourth increase this year and the highest level since November 2011. All four major banks, Commonwealth Bank, NAB, Westpac and ANZ, passed the rise on in full, lifting variable home loan rates from October 9. The move follows August inflation data showing the consumer price index climbing to 4 percent, up from 3.5 percent in July, driven by housing costs and petrol prices.

Israel has accused Foreign Minister Penny Wong of a 'blatant lie' in a speech to the United Nations, straining relations already frayed by Canberra's support for Palestinian statehood. Pacific defence ministers pledge collective action on regional security

Fiji's Defence Minister Pio Tikoduadua closed the South Pacific Defence Ministers' Meeting with a call for collective action, declaring that 'no single country, regardless of its size or capability, can address these issues alone.' The three-day talks, held under the theme 'Securing the Blue Pacific: Collective Responsibility, Resilient Futures,' covered transnational organised crime, climate change and the Pacific Response Group. Tikoduadua said the forum's 'centre of gravity' remained relationships among member states, not a policy document or military capability.
